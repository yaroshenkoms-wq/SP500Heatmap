Common questions

Prices look frozen. Live prices only update during regular U.S. market hours (9:30–16:00 America/New_York, weekdays). Outside that window, prices show the last regular-session price on purpose.

Is the data real? Yes — company/sector data, historical prices, and live quotes all come from real market data sources.
