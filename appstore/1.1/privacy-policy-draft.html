Privacy Policy for SP500 Heatmap

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

SP500 Heatmap has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your watchlists, portfolio and settings stay on your device and in your own iCloud. The only data that reaches our server is what is needed to deliver push notifications you turn on: a push token, your app language, your time zone and the alert rules you create.

What the app does

The app displays publicly available market data — stock indices, sectors, market capitalization, prices, fundamentals, news headlines and crypto/ETF data — fetched over HTTPS from our own server, which sources it from Yahoo Finance, Finnhub (US live quotes) and CoinGecko. These requests contain no personal information.

Push notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, the app sends our server an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token, the app's language, your time zone and the alerts you set up (for example, a stock symbol and a price threshold, an index move threshold, or a daily summary). We use this only to send you those notifications and the Live Activity updates you start. It is not linked to your name, email or any account, is not shared with or sold to anyone, and is not used for tracking or advertising. Turning notifications off or deleting the app stops delivery; tokens Apple reports as inactive are deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete your data at the address below.

Data that stays on your device

  • Watchlists, portfolio and settings are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). We cannot see them.
  • AI features (“why is it moving”, market brief, chat) run on your device using Apple's on-device model; your questions do not leave the device.
  • Calendar: if you add an earnings or dividend date to your calendar, the app asks for write-only access and does not read your calendar.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers.

Server logs

Like any server on the internet, our hosting infrastructure may log basic connection metadata (such as IP address and request time) for operation and abuse prevention. These logs are kept for a short time, are not linked to any identity and are not used for tracking or advertising.

Not investment advice

All information in the app, including model portfolios and AI-generated texts, is for information only and is not investment advice.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised “Last updated” date.
